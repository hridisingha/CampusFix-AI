Our features

Everything you need for a safer, smarter campus

Report in seconds

Describe the problem, pick the building, add a photo. Students can file a report from a phone in under a minute.

AI triage

CampusFix suggests a category and priority from your description, so urgent safety problems reach the top of the queue.

Technician tasks

Each technician sees their own assigned work, starts a job, and marks it complete from one list.

Live status tracking

Follow every report from open to resolved with a clear timeline of who did what, and when.

Safety first

Critical reports such as exposed wires or gas smells are flagged for administrators the moment they come in.

Admin insights

See which buildings and categories need attention, and how fast the team is closing issues.

How it works

From a reported problem to a fixed one

1

Report

A student or staff member describes the issue and where it is.

2

Triage

CampusFix suggests a priority and an administrator assigns a technician.

3

Fix

The technician starts the task and records progress on the issue.

4

Resolve

The reporter sees the update and the issue is closed with a full history.

One place for everyone who keeps a university running
